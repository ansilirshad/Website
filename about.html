ABOUT VVS FRESH N FINE

Creating Value. Building Trust. Making Life Better.

VVS Fresh n Fine began its journey in the wholesale segment with a clear ambition — to challenge traditional ways of doing business and create a more professional, transparent and customer-focused approach to the grocery industry.

From its roots in wholesale, VVS Fresh n Fine has grown into both wholesale and supermarket operations in Kerala, serving customers across different segments with pride, reliability and better customer service.

OUR STORY

From wholesale beginnings to a growing retail and wholesale presence.

Our journey is built on the belief that the grocery business can be managed differently — with stronger systems, better service, meaningful relationships and a continuous focus on creating value.

Today, VVS Fresh n Fine brings together wholesale and supermarket retail, serving everyday customer needs while building dependable relationships with suppliers, employees, business partners and other stakeholders.

We believe that sustainable growth comes from serving people well, improving continuously and creating value for everyone connected with our business.
